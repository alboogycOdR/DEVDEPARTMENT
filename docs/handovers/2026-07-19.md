## Traps discovered
- Same-model maker/checker is a silent failure mode, not a loud one: nothing errors when ORCH and a builder share a model, review quality just degrades and the Wave C learning loop starves quietly (no rework findings to mine). Worth remembering next time a new builder unit is added on any of ORCH's own candidate models.
- `git status` showing clean does not mean `.gitattributes` eol rules have actually been applied to committed blobs — `git check-attr` reporting `eol: lf` only means *future* checkouts will normalize; it says nothing about what's already committed. Verify with `git show HEAD:<path>` directly, not just `check-attr`.
